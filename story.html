About Us

We dedicated ourselves to produce products with only the best-in-class materials and exceptional designs.

We are a family-operated Small Cashmere and Pashmina factory based in Nepal. We are the manufacturer, wholesaler, and distributor for over 200 customers around the world. For us, profit isn’t everything—our top priority is 100% customer satisfaction. We welcome orders of all sizes, from small to bulk quantities. Whether you’re a shop, boutique, retailer, wholesale buyer, or online store, we would love to be your trusted supplier.

"At Whole Sale Pashmina, we place great emphasis on maintaining stringent quality control measures. This enables us to offer you superior value at competitive prices, as we firmly believe in supporting your growth and expansion."

Our Craftsmanship & Materials

We take pride in highlighting the artistry of woven and knitwear craftsmanship. Our focus is on producing high-quality cashmere garments primarily using 100% pure organic cashmere yarn. We are also open to working with various cashmere blended yarns, such as cashmere silk and cashmere cotton, among others.
